## [1.0.0] - 2025-08-14
**Initial Public Release – Early Testing Stage**

HighLaunchPad is now in **early public testing**. Most features are fully functional, but some edge cases may need refinement. This release is open-source to enable community contributions.

### 🎨 Features
- **Visual Funnel Builder**: Drag-and-drop editor, mobile-responsive templates, A/B testing, and payment integration.  
- **AI Content Engine**: Email sequence generation, smart sales copy optimization, headline & CTA suggestions, and content ideation for blogs & social media.  
- **Email Marketing Automation**: Visual workflow builder, trigger-based automation, advanced segmentation, and professional email templates.  
- **Affiliate Link Management**: Smart link tracking, automated UTM parameters, performance dashboards, and team collaboration tools.  
- **Smart CRM Dashboard**: Lead scoring, pipeline management, contact enrichment, activity tracking, and form integration.  
- **Notion-Style Content Pad**: Rich text editor, AI writing assistance, template library, seamless integration.  
- **Workflow Automation**: Multi-channel orchestration, custom triggers and actions, integration with popular tools.  

### ⚠️ Early Public Testing Notes
- Platform is functional but may contain bugs or incomplete integrations.  
- Early adopters and contributors are encouraged to provide feedback.  
- Contributions (features, fixes, UI/UX improvements) are welcome via GitHub.
